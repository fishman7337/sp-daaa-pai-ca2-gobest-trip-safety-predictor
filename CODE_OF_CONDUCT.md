# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We aim to make this project welcoming, respectful, and useful for learning. Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate with patience, assume good intent, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

## Expected Behaviour

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different experience levels and learning goals.
- Give constructive, specific feedback.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Avoid sharing private data, credentials, or school submission materials.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Submitting secrets, raw private datasets, or files that violate data-use rules.
- Misrepresenting this educational project as production safety software.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code. Repeated or serious violations may lead to a ban from project spaces.
